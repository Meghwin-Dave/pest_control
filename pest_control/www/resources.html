Pest Guide

Key Pests You’re Most Likely to See.

Start with the high‑impact pests that most frequently affect homes, hospitality, offices and warehouses across the UAE.

Bed Bugs

Nocturnal pests that hide in mattress seams, bed frames and soft furnishings. Often spread through luggage, staff housing and guest turnover.

  • Warning signs: red marks on bedding, bites in lines or clusters.
  • Risks: guest complaints, brand damage, repeated room closures.
View bed bug treatment approach
Cockroaches

Highly resilient pests that thrive in warm, humid and food‑rich environments such as kitchens, dishwashing areas and waste rooms.

  • Warning signs: droppings, egg cases, sightings when lights are turned on.
  • Risks: food safety violations, negative guest reviews, staff concerns.
View cockroach treatment approach
Rodents

Rats and mice that exploit gaps around loading bays, drainage lines and poorly sealed doors, especially in warehouses and food sites.

  • Warning signs: gnaw marks, droppings, noises in ceilings or behind walls.
  • Risks: stock damage, equipment failure, health & safety incidents.

Practical Tips

Simple Steps to Reduce Pest Pressure.

These are the everyday actions that make the biggest difference across homes, F&B sites and commercial buildings.

For homes & residential towers

  • Seal gaps around balcony doors, AC sleeves and service ducts where insects can enter.
  • Keep food areas dry and crumb‑free, especially under appliances and in cupboards.
  • Report early signs of pests to building management before they spread between units.

For commercial & industrial sites

  • Keep back‑of‑house, waste and delivery areas as tidy as front‑of‑house.
  • Log all pest sightings so trends can be identified and treated proactively.
  • Ensure doors, dock levellers and roller shutters close fully with no visible gaps.
